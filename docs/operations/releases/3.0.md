# Release 3.0

Marketing version: 3.0.0. Local build number: 1 for the app and widget extension.
Xcode Cloud assigns the uploaded build number.

This release moves the upstream alert map from its own tab onto a compact card
at the top of the Home screen, so the phone companion has two tabs (Home,
Regions). The major version marks that navigation change. It also fixes map
loading on launch, a misleading "No current data" title in CarPlay after a
weak-signal start, and Siri answers that used older status wording. Free on all
surfaces; no subscription or CarPlay layout changes.

It is the first release shipped through the gated flow in
[release-process.md](../release-process.md): the App Store candidate is built
from the `release` branch after the `v3.0.0` tag passes its checks.

release gating, removal of the unused AI explanation runtime) are recorded in
ADR 0009 and ADR 0010.

## What's New — English

- The alert map now sits at the top of the Home screen, right above your region's status — two tabs instead of three.
- The map loads reliably when you open the app.
- CarPlay keeps showing a recent status on a weak connection instead of "No current data".
- Siri and Shortcuts now describe alert status with the same words as the app and CarPlay.

## Validation and delivery

Follow [release-process.md](../release-process.md#releasing-a-version). For 3.0:

1. The release-prep commit contains this note and the 3.0 `CHANGELOG.md` section; `MARKETING_VERSION` is already `3.0.0` in every target and configuration.
2. Run `just verify` and `just release --check`, and push the release-prep commit as the head of its push.
3. Wait for that commit's "Tests and coverage" run to succeed and for `testflight` to reach it.
4. The owner creates and pushes the annotated tag `v3.0.0` on that commit. Tags are never moved (the 2.8 policy of moving a tag before publication no longer applies).
5. Confirm the Release workflow ends with `release -> v3.0.0` and Xcode Cloud "App Store candidate (release tag)" produces the build.
6. Check on a device before submission: map card on Home in light and dark appearance, map loading after a cold launch, CarPlay title after launching on a weak connection, and a Siri status answer in English, Ukrainian, and Russian.
7. Select the build in App Store Connect, paste the What's New copy above, review the localized notes, and submit.
