# Release 3.1

Marketing version 3.1.0, minimum iOS 27. The first TestFlight round (`tf-3.1.0-1`) was local build
number 1; the second (`tf-3.1.0-2`) was local build number 2 for the app, the tests and the widget
extension; the third (`tf-3.1.0-3`) is local build number 3. The `BUILD` of a `tf-3.1.0-BUILD` tag
counts TestFlight rounds. Xcode Cloud assigns the build number that testers see.

3.1 is the first feature update after 3.0. It closes the three product questions left open at the
3.0 submission: Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast, the Live Activity's stale colour, and the Control Center
control during Stay Alert. The fold glass on the Status screen, the first lab item under core's
"The lab" clause, shipped to TestFlight in builds 1 and 2 and was removed before submission on the
owner's decision of 2026-09-24 (brief [`../../tasks/fold-glass-retirement.md`](../../tasks/fold-glass-retirement.md)).

## What's New — English

- Kyiv now shows "Stay Alert" whenever Kyiv Oblast is under alert.
- The Control Center control matches the widget: a yellow warning for "Stay Alert" and a clock when the data is old.
- The Live Activity shows old data in grey, says when an alert may be outdated, and has a Refresh button that checks again without opening the app.

## Device checklist for the first TestFlight round

No results were reported for builds 1 and 2, so this list is also due on build 3, together with
the build 3 checklist below. These checks need a real device:

- Live Activity: the grey clock on old data. Control Center: the yellow triangle during Stay Alert,
  the clock on old data, and the tint.
- Kyiv: with Kyiv Oblast alone under alert, Kyiv city is yellow on the phone, CarPlay, the widget
  and Siri.

## Device checklist for build 3

The build 2 checklist below moved unchanged to build 3 because build 2 was not checked before the
removal.

The Live Activity and widget changes need a real device and time:

- During an alert, start the Live Activity and lock the phone. After 15 min without opening the
  app, the red activity says "May be outdated. Open the app." on the Lock Screen and, with a paired
  watch, in the Smart Stack.
- Tap Refresh on the Lock Screen and in the expanded Dynamic Island while the alert is still on:
  the app does not open, the time updates and the marker goes away.
- After the alert ends, tap Refresh: the activity disappears without opening the app.
- With the network off, tap Refresh: the activity stays red and marked stale.
- A Home Screen widget left for 10 min or more says "Open the app to update"; before that it says
  "Last known".
- CarPlay: the Live Activity and the Status screen behave as in build 1.
- Details has no fold glass switch, and Home does not move when the phone tilts.

## Known limitations

- An all-clear seen by Refresh removes the Live Activity at once (the existing immediate
  dismissal), without showing "All Clear" first.
- The Live Activity still cannot re-check on its own: without a tap, CarPlay or opening the app it
  keeps its last content, now marked stale after 15 min. Scheduled notifications (path (b)) need a
  `core.md` amendment and are not in 3.1.0.
- iPhone Duo is not checked. Its simulator needs a 27.1 runtime that Xcode 27.2 beta cannot
  download. See the backlog.
