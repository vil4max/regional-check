# Release 3.1

Marketing version 3.1.0, local build number 1 for the app and the widget extension, minimum
iOS 27. The `BUILD` of a `tf-3.1.0-BUILD` tag counts TestFlight rounds. Xcode Cloud assigns the
build number that testers see.

3.1 is the first feature update after 3.0. It adds the fold glass on the Status screen, which is
the first lab item under core's "The lab" clause. It also closes the three product questions left
open at the 3.0 submission: Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast, the Live Activity's stale colour, and the
Control Center control during Stay Alert.

## What's New — English

- Fold glass: the Status screen stays in place as you tilt your phone, seen through frosted glass. You can turn it off in Details. It is off with Reduce Motion or Low Power Mode.
- Kyiv now shows "Stay Alert" whenever Kyiv Oblast is under alert.
- The Control Center control matches the widget: a yellow warning for "Stay Alert" and a clock when the data is old.
- The Live Activity shows old data in grey.

## Device checklist for the first TestFlight round

The simulator has no motion, so these checks need a real device:

- Fold glass: frame time on Home while tilting, battery over 10 minutes with Home open, the status
  hero readable at the maximum tilt, and no motion permission prompt or crash.
- Fold glass: landscape tilt direction in both landscape orientations; rotating the phone
  recalibrates the zero pose; Low Power Mode and Reduce Motion make Home flat; the Details switch
  turns it off and on.
- Live Activity: the grey clock on old data. Control Center: the yellow triangle during Stay Alert,
  the clock on old data, and the tint.
- Kyiv: with Kyiv Oblast alone under alert, Kyiv city is yellow on the phone, CarPlay, the widget
  and Siri.

## Known limitations

- The App Store screenshots do not change: static captures show Home flat.
- The fold glass uses SwiftUI's rotation, blur and dim. A Metal shader needs the Metal Toolchain
  component, which this Mac and CI do not install; it is a later lab slice (owner, 2026-09-23).
- iPhone Duo is not checked. Its simulator needs a 27.1 runtime that Xcode 27.2 beta cannot
  download. See the backlog.
