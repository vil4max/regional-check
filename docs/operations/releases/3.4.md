# Drive Check 3.4

## 3.4.0

Feature release. Specs: [MAP-sync](../../../specs/MAP-sync/spec.md),
[LIVEACT-glance](../../../specs/LIVEACT-glance/spec.md),
[STATUS-reason](../../../specs/STATUS-reason/spec.md). Pull requests: vil4max/regional-check#38, #39
and the STATUS-reason pull request.

### What changed

- **Live Activity**:
  - "Started 12 minutes ago" from the alert's start time in the Ubilling response, and the data
    time "Updated: 08:54" in every state.
  - The green all-clear says "All clear at 08:54", the time of the first response that showed the
    region clear.
  - Shown on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, in StandBy (large layout), on the CarPlay
    Dashboard and on Apple Watch. The `.small` view has no button.
  - VoiceOver reads each view as one sentence.
- **One symbol per status everywhere**: the siren and the shield with a check, on the Status tab,
  the widget, Control Center and the Live Activity.
- **Radar**: the radar is back on the Status tab around the status symbol.
- **Reason line**: under the region, one line says why the status is what it is: "Alert nearby:
  <names>" while the region is quiet, "Alert since <time>" during its own alert. The "Nearby" and
  "Regions under alert N of 25" rows are gone; the map opens the region list.
- **Live Activity fix**: a green all-clear left on screen no longer blocks the red activity of a
  new alert (#39).
- **Inline map**:
  - It follows the status: it reloads when the regions under alert change and after a pull that
    fetched a status, and it bypasses the local HTTP cache.
  - It shows its load time.
  - While it loads, a grey outline with a shimmer stands in its place.

### Server

drivecheck-api#12 sends the same start time in Live Activity pushes, so an activity the server
starts shows it before the app opens. Without that deploy, activities the app starts still show it.

### Known open points

- The minimal Dynamic Island view shows the symbol only, with no minutes: no system format writes
  bare minutes without ticking seconds (LIVEACT-glance plan, open point 8).
- Not checked on a device before this note: a real alert with the start line, the CarPlay
  Dashboard sizes, VoiceOver.

### App Privacy answers

Unchanged from 3.3: the start and all-clear times come from the public alert feed, and no new data
leaves the device.

### App Review notes

No new paragraph. The 3.3 notes on notifications and Live Activities still describe the app.

### What's New — English

- The Live Activity shows when the alert in your region started and when the data was updated, on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, in StandBy, on CarPlay and on Apple Watch. After the all-clear it shows the time of the all-clear.
- The same status symbols everywhere: the app, widgets and the Live Activity.
- The alert map follows the status and shows when it was updated.
- Under the status, one line says why: an alert nearby, or when the alert in your region started.

### What's New — Ukrainian

- Live Activity показує, коли почалася тривога у вашому регіоні та коли оновилися дані: на екрані блокування, у Dynamic Island, у режимі StandBy, у CarPlay та на Apple Watch. Після відбою показує час відбою.
- Однакові символи статусу всюди: у застосунку, віджетах і Live Activity.
- Мапа тривог оновлюється разом зі статусом і показує час оновлення.
- Під статусом один рядок пояснює чому: тривога поруч або коли почалася тривога у вашому регіоні.

### What's New — Russian

- Live Activity показывает, когда началась тревога в вашем регионе и когда обновились данные: на экране блокировки, в Dynamic Island, в режиме StandBy, в CarPlay и на Apple Watch. После отбоя показывает время отбоя.
- Одинаковые символы статуса везде: в приложении, виджетах и Live Activity.
- Карта тревог обновляется вместе со статусом и показывает время обновления.
- Под статусом одна строка объясняет почему: тревога рядом или когда началась тревога в вашем регионе.
