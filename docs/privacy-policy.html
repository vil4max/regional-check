Drive Check Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Drive Check (“the App”) is a minimal CarPlay and iPhone utility by Maksim Vilchevskiy.

What we collect

The App uses your device location only while the App is in use, to determine which region to show. Your precise location is processed on device and is not uploaded to our servers; only the name of your region derived from it is sent, when you switch on alert notifications or while Live Activities are on (below). We do not create accounts, and we do not collect name, email, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Alert notifications and Live Activities

If you switch on alert notifications, the App sends your device’s push notification token, the last region it saw, the language (English, Ukrainian or Russian) and whether it is a test build to the Drive Check server (drivecheck.vil4max.com), so the server can notify you when an alert in that region starts and when it ends. If Live Activities are on, the App also sends the server the Live Activity tokens Apple issues for this device, with the same region, language and build environment, so the server can start, update and end the Live Activity during an alert, even if notifications are off. The server also keeps the time it last heard from your device and deletes a token that was not refreshed for 60 days. Switching the notification off deletes its token from the server; switching Live Activities off, in the App or in iOS Settings, deletes the Live Activity tokens. We do not send your coordinates, and we do not link this to a name, account or advertising identifier. Apple delivers the notification through its push service.

Subscriptions

Optional Drive Check Pro subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple. Apple processes payment; we do not receive your full payment card details. Subscription status may be cached on device to unlock Pro features after verification.

Live Activities

If Live Activities are on, the App may show your region’s status on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island while Drive Check is active in the foreground or on CarPlay. When an alert starts in your region, the Drive Check server may also start the Live Activity, keep it current every few minutes, and end it after the all-clear, while the App is not running. You can turn Live Activities off on the App’s Settings tab or in iOS Settings.

Third-party data

When you open or refresh, the App requests a public regional notice status from a third-party data source over the network. That request is needed to display status for your region. We do not sell data and we do not use analytics SDKs or ads.

Apple may provide aggregated App Store analytics and crash reports to the developer through App Store Connect and Xcode. We do not receive personal data through those channels.

Data sharing and tracking

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data for tracking across apps or websites. We do not use your data for advertising.

Retention

The App may keep the last selected region on device for convenience. Clearing the App or reinstalling removes local data.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
